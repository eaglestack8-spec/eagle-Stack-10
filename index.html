About Our Organization

A Haven of Compassion, Education & Dignity in Ooty

Established in 1988 in the hill station of Ootacamund (Ooty), Tamil Nadu, SMYRNA Fellowship Trust was founded on the principles of unconditional love and practical service to society's most vulnerable.

Over the past three decades, Smyrna has expanded from a modest shelter into a comprehensive charitable ecosystem. We operate a residential home for orphaned and destitute children, a tranquil senior citizen sanctuary ("Smyrna Haven"), a vocational training center for differently-abled youth, and mobile health outreach units serving indigenous tribal communities across the Nilgiris district.

Core Pillars of Smyrna

Our Dedicated Initiatives in Ooty

Every program at Smyrna is designed with extreme care, ensuring long-term empowerment, dignity, and personal growth for every individual we serve.

Child Shelter & Education

Providing a safe, loving residential home for orphaned, abandoned, and underprivileged children in the Nilgiris. We offer formal schooling, tuition support, nutritious meals, healthcare, and psychological counseling.

Impact: 120+ Resident Children Supported Annually

Smyrna Haven Elder Care

A peaceful sanctuary nestled in Ooty for destitute and destitute elderly citizens. We offer round-the-clock geriatric care, doctor checkups, balanced diets, comfortable warm beds, and a family environment where elders live with pride.

Impact: 60+ Resident Senior Citizens

Special Needs Vocational Center

Empowering specially-abled young adults with tailored vocational skills — including loom weaving, handcrafts, tailoring, candle making, and computer literacy — fostering self-reliance and dignified livelihoods.

Impact: 45+ Youth Enrolled in Vocational Crafts

Tribal Mobile Healthcare Units

Our mobile health clinics traverse narrow mountain roads across the Nilgiris tea gardens to reach indigenous Kota, Toda, Kurumba, and Badaga villages, delivering free doctor consultations, diagnostic tests, and vital medications.

Impact: 5,000+ Tribal Patients Served Annually

Women Empowerment & Micro-Grants

Supporting rural women in the Nilgiris district through sewing machine distribution, self-help group mentorship, micro-business guidance, and emergency financial aid during harsh winter seasons.

Impact: 300+ Women Empowered
